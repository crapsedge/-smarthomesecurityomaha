1. Who We Are

Smart Home Security & Internet is operated by Murrays Empire LLC, a Nebraska limited liability company.

We provide independent marketing, customer-intake, informational and referral services relating to internet, home security and connected-home products and services.

We are not an internet service provider, telecommunications carrier, alarm monitoring company, equipment manufacturer, installation contractor or payment processor.

Actual services are supplied and managed by the applicable independent providers.

2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information collected through:

  • Our website.
  • Internet service inquiries.
  • Home security update requests.
  • General contact forms.
  • Referral and promotional inquiries.
  • Related email, telephone, text message and other communications.

This policy concerns information handled by Murrays Empire LLC. Independent service providers may maintain separate privacy policies and customer agreements.

6. Information Sharing

Internet Service Providers and Referral Partners

When you submit an internet inquiry and provide the applicable authorization, we may share information relevant to your request with participating referral partners and the applicable internet service provider.

This information may include your name, contact information, service address, requested plan, contact preferences and other details necessary to assist with the requested service.

Information sharing may enable availability reviews, service quotations, enrollment assistance, installation scheduling and referral tracking.

Home Security Updates

While home security enrollment is temporarily unavailable, information submitted for security updates may be used to respond to your request and provide requested availability updates.

A request for security updates does not by itself authorize forwarding your information to a security provider for enrollment. Additional authorization will be obtained where necessary before such a referral.

Business and Technology Services

Information may also be processed by technology and business service providers that assist with hosting, communications, form delivery, security, recordkeeping and other necessary business functions.

Compensated Information Sharing

Because we operate a compensated referral business, personal information may be provided to relevant third parties in connection with referral activities for which we receive compensation.

Certain compensated disclosures may qualify as a sale or other regulated transfer of personal information under applicable state privacy laws. Where applicable, we will honor legally required privacy choices and opt-out rights.

7. Referral Compensation

Murrays Empire LLC may receive commissions, referral fees, marketing payments or other compensation for qualifying referrals.

Relevant information may be exchanged with participating providers or referral partners to determine whether a referral was received, approved, installed, activated or otherwise qualified for compensation or promotional benefits.

Referral compensation does not itself establish a payment obligation between the customer and Murrays Empire LLC.

Our customer inquiry and contact forms require an affirmative acknowledgment before submission.
