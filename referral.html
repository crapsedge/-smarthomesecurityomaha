Qualification Rules

What makes a referral eligible for the $100 payment?

These rules are here so the referrer, referred customer, and Smart Home Security all know exactly how a referral is credited.

1

The referral must be identifiable.

The referred customer should provide the referrer's full name in the “Referred By” field when submitting their request. A referral must be recorded before the referred customer's setup is completed.

2

The referred person must complete a qualifying setup.

A lead, inquiry, phone conversation, quote, application, appointment, or scheduled installation does not by itself earn a referral payment. The security setup must actually be completed and confirmed.

3

Only one referrer may receive credit for the same setup.

If more than one person claims the same referred customer, Smart Home Security will generally use the first valid referral information recorded in its records before the setup was completed.

4

No self-referrals.

A customer cannot refer themselves in order to receive a $100 referral payment for their own security setup.

5

Payment information must be accurate.

The referrer is responsible for providing accurate information needed to receive payment through Cash App, Venmo, or Apple Pay. Incorrect or incomplete payment information may delay delivery.

6

The referral cannot be fraudulent or manipulated.

Fake identities, false customer information, duplicate accounts, fabricated installations, manipulated referrals, or other abuse of the program are not eligible for payment.

When A Referral Does Not Qualify

A referral payment is earned only after a qualifying completed setup.

The following situations do not create an obligation to pay the $100 referral payment.

The customer only requests information. Filling out the website form or requesting pricing is not a completed setup.
The customer schedules but does not complete setup. A scheduled installation or appointment alone does not qualify.
The customer cancels before setup is completed. No referral payment is earned if the qualifying setup never occurs.
The provider cannot complete the setup. Provider approval, service availability, property requirements, or other conditions may prevent setup.
The same customer was already validly referred. Only one referral payment is available for the same qualifying customer setup.
The referral information is fraudulent. False, manipulated, duplicate, or abusive referrals are not eligible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common referral questions.

When do I actually earn the $100?

You earn a qualifying referral payment after the person you referred completes their qualifying Smart Home Security setup and Smart Home Security receives confirmation of that completed setup.

Does the person just have to fill out the form?

No. A submitted form is only an inquiry. The referred customer must complete a qualifying security setup before a referral payment becomes eligible.

What if they schedule an installation but cancel?

If the setup is not completed, the referral does not qualify for the $100 payment.

When will I receive my money?

A qualifying referral payment is sent within 10 business days after Smart Home Security receives confirmation that the referred customer's setup has been completed.

How can I get paid?

Referral payments may be sent using Cash App, Venmo, or Apple Pay. You will need to provide accurate payment information.

What if two people say they referred the same person?

Only one person may receive the referral payment for the same qualifying customer setup. Smart Home Security will generally use the first valid referral recorded in its records before the customer's setup was completed.

What if the customer forgets to enter my name?

The safest way to protect your referral credit is to make sure the customer enters your full name in the “Referred By” field when they first submit their request. Referral information must be recorded before the setup is completed.

Can I refer myself?

No. Self-referrals do not qualify for the $100 referral payment.

Can I refer more than one person?

Yes. Each separate referral must independently satisfy the program requirements before a $100 referral payment is earned.

Do referral payments have tax consequences?

Referral payments may be taxable income. Each recipient is responsible for their own tax obligations. Smart Home Security or Murrays Empire LLC may request tax or identification information when required by applicable law.

Full Program Terms

Smart Home Security Referral Program Terms

Effective Date: August 25, 2026

1. Program Operator

The Smart Home Security Referral Program is operated by Murrays Empire LLC under the Smart Home Security name.

2. Referral Payment

A qualifying referrer may receive a $100 referral payment for a referred customer who completes a qualifying Smart Home Security setup, subject to the requirements stated on this page.

3. What Counts as a Completed Setup

For purposes of the referral program, a completed setup occurs when the applicable provider confirms that the referred customer's qualifying security setup or installation has been completed and the applicable service or account has been activated or otherwise marked complete by the provider.

An inquiry, quote, application, approval, equipment order, appointment, scheduled installation, signed paperwork, or other preliminary step is not itself a completed setup.

4. Referral Identification

The referred customer should identify the referrer by entering the referrer's full name in the “Referred By” field when submitting a request through the Smart Home Security website.

Referral information must be recorded before the referred customer's setup is completed. Smart Home Security may decline a referral claim first made after completion when there is no prior record connecting the referrer to that customer.

5. Duplicate Referral Claims

Only one referral payment may be issued for the same qualifying customer setup.

When multiple people claim the same customer, Smart Home Security will generally use the first valid referral information recorded in its records before the customer's setup was completed.

6. Self-Referrals

Customers may not refer themselves or use another identity, account, household member, or payment account solely to create a referral payment for their own setup.

7. Payment Timing

A qualifying $100 referral payment will be sent within 10 business days after Smart Home Security receives confirmation that the referred customer's qualifying setup has been completed.

Business days generally mean Monday through Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays.

8. Payment Methods

Referral payments may be issued through Cash App, Venmo, or Apple Pay. The referrer is responsible for providing accurate information necessary to receive payment.

Smart Home Security is not responsible for delays caused solely by incorrect payment information, unavailable third-party payment accounts, account restrictions, or technical issues involving the recipient's selected payment service.

9. Canceled or Uncompleted Setups

No referral payment is earned if the referred customer's qualifying setup is never completed.

This includes situations in which the customer cancels before completion, does not appear for installation, does not meet provider requirements, is outside an available service area, or the provider otherwise cannot complete the setup.

10. Fraud and Abuse

Smart Home Security may deny a referral payment associated with fraud, fabricated customer information, duplicate or fake accounts, manipulated referrals, false installation claims, identity misuse, or other attempts to improperly obtain a referral payment.

11. Referrers Are Not Sales Representatives

Participation in this customer referral program does not by itself create an employment relationship, partnership, agency relationship, franchise, joint venture, or authority to act on behalf of Smart Home Security, Murrays Empire LLC, or any service provider.

Unless separately authorized in writing, a referrer may not:

  • Enter into contracts on behalf of Smart Home Security;
  • Collect customer payments on behalf of Smart Home Security;
  • Guarantee approval or installation;
  • Change advertised package terms or pricing;
  • Guarantee security outcomes;
  • Make promises on behalf of a provider; or
  • Represent themselves as an employee or authorized agent solely because they participate in this referral program.

12. Public Promotions and Recommendations

A person who publicly recommends, endorses, or promotes Smart Home Security while expecting or seeking a $100 referral payment should clearly disclose that financial relationship in connection with the recommendation.

The disclosure should be easy for an ordinary person to notice and understand.

13. Taxes

Referral payments may constitute taxable income to the recipient. Each recipient is responsible for determining and satisfying their own tax obligations.

Murrays Empire LLC may request identification, payment, or tax documentation when reasonably necessary or required by applicable law.

14. Privacy

Information collected for referral administration may be used to verify the referral, communicate with the parties involved, process payment, prevent fraud, maintain business records, and comply with applicable requirements.

Additional information concerning website data practices is available in the Smart Home Security Privacy Policy .

15. Program Changes

Smart Home Security may modify, suspend, or discontinue the referral program prospectively.

A program change will not be used solely to avoid paying a referral payment that was already properly earned under the rules in effect when the qualifying setup was completed.

16. Verification

Smart Home Security may reasonably verify customer identity, referral records, completed setup status, payment information, and other information necessary to determine whether a referral qualifies.

17. Questions or Referral Disputes

If you believe a qualifying referral was not properly credited, contact Smart Home Security as soon as possible and provide the referrer's name and enough information to identify the referred customer.

Smart Home Security may review its website submission records, provider confirmation, referral information, and other available records to resolve the issue.
