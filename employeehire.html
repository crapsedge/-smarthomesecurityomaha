What the job actually is

Simple enough to explain in one sentence.

Your job:

Find people who may want home security, start an honest conversation, identify what they need, and move interested people into the official signup process.

A normal conversation

  1. Ask: “What are you trying to protect — front door, outside cameras, or the whole home?”
  2. Learn: renter/homeowner, ZIP code, camera interest, timing, and best contact method.
  3. Explain: only the current approved offer and the fact that final terms are confirmed by the applicable provider.
  4. Submit: put the lead into the official Rep Lead form using your Rep ID.
  5. Follow up: remind the customer to complete the provider process, but do not pressure or mislead them.

What you are NOT hired to do

  • Install cameras, alarm panels, wiring, or other security equipment.
  • Provide monitoring.
  • Approve customers for service.
  • Change provider agreements.
  • Give legal, tax, electrical, insurance, or credit advice to customers.
Installation work is different from sales. Nebraska has licensing rules for electrical/security-related installation work. This sales portal does not authorize any rep to install equipment.

Door-to-door rules

Read this before knocking on one door.

Omaha: do not knock until the permit is confirmed.

Omaha treats a person going dwelling-to-dwelling and offering services now or in the future as a peddler. A permit is required, and door-to-door permit holders receive a photo ID badge.

Before going door-to-door

If a customer signs at home

Home solicitation/cooling-off laws may give the consumer three business days to cancel and require specific written notices. The actual contracting seller/provider must provide the legally required contract and cancellation documents. A rep must never remove, hide, alter, or tell the customer to ignore those rights.

Easy rule:

If you are at somebody's house and a sale is about to be signed, stop improvising. Use only the provider's official contract process and cancellation disclosures.

Calls, texts, and online selling

Phone and electronic selling have separate compliance risks.

No random mass calling or texting

A rep may not use purchased consumer lists, robocalls, prerecorded calls, automated marketing texts, bulk SMS, or similar systems unless Murrays Empire LLC has specifically approved the campaign and compliance process in writing.

Live calls

  • Identify Smart Home Security and the sales purpose promptly.
  • Do not call a person who has told Smart Home Security not to call them.
  • Do not call outside permitted calling hours. Federal telemarketing guidance generally restricts covered outbound telemarketing calls to 8:00 a.m.–9:00 p.m. local time at the person called, unless an applicable exception or consent applies.
  • Company-approved campaigns must use required Do-Not-Call procedures and list screening.
  • Never spoof caller ID or impersonate another company.

Texts and automated technology

Automated or prerecorded marketing calls and texts can require specific consent under federal law. A customer giving a phone number for human follow-up does not automatically mean a rep can enroll that number in robocalls or bulk marketing texts.

Worker classification note: Nebraska's special direct-seller exclusion discussed in this portal is for qualifying sales performed primarily in person. Primarily telephone/electronic selling is expressly excluded from that particular Nebraska direct-seller provision. That does not automatically decide worker status, but it means the Company should reassess classification before using a primarily remote sales model.

INDEPENDENT SALES REPRESENTATIVE AGREEMENT

Parties. This Agreement is between Murrays Empire LLC, operator of the Smart Home Security marketing brand (“Company”), and the person identified in the electronic signature section (“Representative”).

1. Purpose and limited role

Representative may identify prospective customers, discuss Company-approved marketing offers, collect only authorized lead information, submit leads through the Company's approved process, and assist prospects with follow-up. Smart Home Security is a marketing/customer-intake/referral business. Applicable third-party providers control final eligibility, equipment, installation, monitoring, billing, warranties, service agreements, cancellations, and other provider terms.

2. No authority to bind the Company or provider

Representative may not sign or modify an agreement for the Company or a provider; promise approval; guarantee pricing, equipment, installation dates, monitoring, contract length, credit requirements, financing, or promotions; collect money; or represent that Representative personally installs or monitors security systems unless separately authorized in writing.

3. Compensation

The current commission is $100 for each qualifying completed installation properly attributed to Representative before installation. A commission is earned only after: (a) the prospect is valid and properly attributed to Representative; (b) the customer completes a qualifying installation; (c) the transaction is not fraudulent, duplicate, fabricated, self-dealing, or otherwise disqualified; and (d) Murrays Empire LLC receives the corresponding provider compensation. Earned commissions are ordinarily paid within seven business days after these conditions are confirmed, subject to payment-system processing and applicable law.

4. Public referral reward does not automatically stack

When Representative receives the sales commission for a customer, Representative is not also entitled to the separate public referral reward for that same customer unless Company approves the additional payment in writing.

5. Independent manner of work

If this relationship is lawfully treated as independent contracting, Representative chooses Representative's lawful prospecting schedule and methods, supplies ordinary personal work tools and transportation, bears ordinary personal business expenses, receives no guaranteed hours or minimum compensation, and may perform services for other non-conflicting businesses subject to Representative's own legal and contractual obligations. Company may set brand, legal-compliance, customer-data, truthful-advertising, and provider requirements without promising that these facts alone determine worker classification.

6. No guaranteed leads or income

Company does not guarantee prospects, appointments, hours, sales, installations, income, or continued opportunity.

7. Truthful sales and approved materials

Representative must use truthful, current, Company-approved information. Representative may not: create fake urgency or scarcity; misstate crime information; impersonate a provider, government agency, law-enforcement officer, utility, competitor, or existing security company; hide material terms; create fake reviews; or change required disclosures.

8. Promotions and pricing

Representative may advertise or state a promotion, monthly price, startup fee, credit requirement, contract statement, equipment quantity, gift card, or referral reward only when it appears in current Company-approved material and is applicable to the customer. Provider eligibility and final terms control where applicable.

9. Door-to-door compliance

Representative may not conduct door-to-door solicitation in Omaha or another jurisdiction until all required permits, badges, registrations, and Company authorization are confirmed. Representative must respect no-soliciting signs and leave immediately when asked. Representative must not interfere with any legally required cooling-off/cancellation notice or consumer right.

10. Calls, texts, and electronic outreach

Representative may not use purchased consumer lists, robocalls, prerecorded calls, automatic dialing, bulk marketing texts, caller-ID spoofing, or other automated consumer outreach without Company's prior written approval of the specific campaign and compliance process. Representative must honor do-not-call requests and follow approved calling-hour, consent, and list-screening procedures.

11. Customer privacy and security

Representative may collect only information requested through approved Company systems. Representative may not request or store: Social Security numbers, bank credentials, payment PINs, account passwords, alarm codes, Wi-Fi passwords, or other prohibited sensitive information. Customer information may be used only for authorized Company activity and may not be sold, copied for personal use, or disclosed except as authorized.

12. No installation authority

This Agreement authorizes sales/referral activity only. Representative is not authorized to install, wire, service, repair, or alter security or electrical equipment unless separately qualified, licensed, engaged, and authorized for that work.

13. Lead ownership and attribution

Company records control lead attribution. Representative should submit a prospect through the official Rep Lead form before provider submission or installation. Duplicate, previously existing, reassigned, invalid, or improperly acquired leads may be denied commission. Representative may not use another business's confidential lead list or customer database without lawful authorization.

14. Fraud, misconduct, and commission reversals

No commission is owed for fraud, fabricated leads, identity misuse, self-dealing designed to obtain a commission, prohibited solicitation, or material misrepresentation. If Company has already paid a commission and the provider later reverses Company compensation because of Representative's fraud or material misconduct, Company may recover or offset the related commission to the extent allowed by law. Ordinary customer cancellation not caused by Representative misconduct does not automatically create a personal debt unless a separate written commission schedule expressly provides otherwise.

15. Taxes and W-9

If Representative is lawfully treated as a nonemployee, Representative is responsible for Representative's taxes and must provide accurate taxpayer information through the Company's approved secure W-9 process. Company will issue required information returns when applicable. If the relationship is legally required to be treated as employment, applicable payroll and employment rules control instead.

W-9 and taxes

This part is intentionally separate because a W-9 can contain an SSN or EIN.

Never type an SSN or EIN into the application, agreement, lead form, Facebook message, text message, or ordinary email.

What happens after approval

  1. Company sends secure W-9 instructions. The rep completes the official IRS W-9 or a compliant secure electronic W-9.
  2. The W-9 goes to Murrays Empire LLC. It is kept in business records. It is not mailed to the IRS just because the rep starts.
  3. Company tracks payments. Commission payments are recorded during the year.
  4. Information reporting. For payments made in 2026, the IRS generally requires Form 1099-NEC for reportable nonemployee service compensation of $2,000 or more, subject to applicable rules and exceptions.

Why the W-9 is not built into this normal Netlify form

The IRS allows electronic W-9 systems, but the system must do more than simply email a form submission. It generally must preserve exactly what was submitted, document submission access, reasonably verify the submitter, reproduce a hard copy, and use an electronic signature with the W-9 perjury certification. Use a dedicated secure W-9/tax-document workflow instead.

Questions people will ask

Plain-English answers.

Am I an employee?

Not automatically. Applying through a page named employeehire does not decide legal status. The Company must use the correct structure based on how the work will actually be done.

Am I automatically a 1099 contractor if I sign the Agreement?

No. A contract label does not override the real facts. The Company uses the independent-rep agreement only when it believes that structure is appropriate.

Do I get $100 for every lead?

No. The current $100 sales commission is for a qualifying completed installation that meets all commission conditions.

Can I work another job?

The independent model is intended to allow other non-conflicting work, but you must obey any lawful agreement you already have with another company.

Can I sell on Facebook?

Only after approval, using truthful Company-approved information. Primarily electronic selling can also affect worker-classification analysis, so the Company needs to know your main sales method.

Can I knock doors?

Not until Company approval and all required local permits/badges are confirmed. Omaha requires a peddler permit for covered door-to-door service solicitation.

Can I collect a customer's card?

No, not unless the Company gives you a specific secure process and written authorization. Never write down card numbers, PINs, or bank credentials.

Can I promise the $100 customer gift card?

Only if it is still in the current approved offer and the customer meets the written promotion requirements. Never change the timing or qualification rules.

Who installs the equipment?

The applicable third-party provider controls installation and related provider services. The sales-rep agreement does not authorize installation.

When do I get paid?

Under the current schedule, after the qualifying installation is confirmed and Murrays Empire LLC has received the corresponding provider compensation, ordinarily within seven business days after those conditions are confirmed.

Do I get the public referral $100 too?

Normally no. The rep commission and public referral reward do not automatically stack on the same customer.

What if a customer cancels?

If the installation never qualifies, there is no sales commission. If a commission was already paid and the provider later reverses payment because of rep fraud or material misconduct, the Agreement allows a lawful offset or recovery. Ordinary cancellation is treated according to the written commission terms.

Can a customer cancel a sale signed at home?

Possibly. Federal and Nebraska cooling-off/home-solicitation laws can apply. Use only the actual provider's authorized contract process and required cancellation notices.

Can I text people I find online?

Do not run bulk or automated consumer marketing yourself. Use only Company-approved outreach methods and consent/compliance procedures.

What information should I never collect?

SSNs, bank credentials, payment PINs, passwords, alarm codes, or other prohibited sensitive information.
