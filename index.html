Common questions

Before you request your setup.

Do I have to be a homeowner?

No. Homeowners and renters can submit a request. Final eligibility and installation details depend on the property and provider requirements.

What information do you need from me?

The request form asks for your full name, service address, email, phone number, and the security package you are interested in.

Can I get more camera coverage?

The packages shown provide different starting levels of camera coverage. Additional equipment availability can be confirmed during setup.

Who provides the final security service?

Smart Home Security is operated by Murrays Empire LLC and handles marketing and customer intake. Installation, monitoring, equipment fulfillment, billing, and final service terms are handled by the applicable third-party security provider.

Are the prices guaranteed for every property?

The listed amounts are the package prices being advertised. Final equipment, eligibility, availability, taxes, provider terms, and any applicable requirements are confirmed before activation.
