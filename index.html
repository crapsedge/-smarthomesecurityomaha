Frequently Asked Questions

Is fiber internet available at my address?

Availability depends on your service address. You are welcome to request an address review through our inquiry form.

Do you offer internet in other states?

Internet options may be available outside Nebraska. Submit your service address and state so we can review available options in your area.

How much does internet cost?

The advertised 1 Gig plan is $40 per month with a qualifying modem or $50 per month without one. The advertised 2 Gig plan is $50 per month with a qualifying modem or $70 per month without one. Availability and final pricing depend on your address, equipment and applicable service terms.

What gift card promotions are available?

The advertised 1 Gig promotion includes a $50 gift card with qualifying service. The advertised 2 Gig promotion includes a $200 gift card with qualifying service. Promotional eligibility, service-period requirements and redemption terms apply.

When would I receive my gift card?

Gift card timing depends on the applicable promotion, eligibility requirements, qualifying service period and redemption instructions. These terms will be confirmed before enrollment.

Who provides my internet service?

Participating internet providers supply, manage and bill for internet service. Murrays Empire LLC assists with independent referrals and customer inquiries.

Is home security currently available?

Home security enrollment is temporarily unavailable. You are welcome to request information about future options.

Does requesting information create an obligation?

No. Completing our inquiry form does not create a service order, purchase agreement or payment obligation.

Does Murrays Empire LLC receive referral compensation?

Murrays Empire LLC may receive compensation for qualifying referrals through participating service providers or referral partners.

How is my information used?

Your inquiry information is used to respond to your request and, when necessary and authorized, assist with reviewing your requested service. Additional information is available in our Privacy Policy .

Can I withdraw my consent to be contacted?

Yes. You may request that we stop contacting you by using our Contact page .
