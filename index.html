Good To Know

Frequently asked questions.

Simple answers before you get started.

Is home internet currently available?

We are accepting inquiries for home internet options in the Omaha area. Specific availability must be confirmed at your service address by a participating provider; submitting a request does not guarantee service.

What are the prices and internet speeds?

Prices, speed tiers, equipment fees, promotional credits, and other terms depend on the provider and address. We will only present a specific offer after the provider's current terms have been confirmed.

Can I switch from my current internet company?

You can submit an inquiry if you already have internet and want to explore different options. Whether a switch is available depends on the providers serving your address and their eligibility rules.

Do I have to buy anything to submit the form?

No. Submitting an availability inquiry does not place an order or create a service contract. Any final application, fees, installation, or service agreement would be handled with the provider.

Is home security still offered?

Not at the moment. New home security requests and applications are temporarily paused while we update our service arrangements. This internet request form is for internet inquiries only.

Who actually provides the internet service?

Smart Home Security & Internet is a marketing and referral resource operated by Murrays Empire LLC. Participating third-party internet providers determine coverage, install or arrange service, bill customers, and set the final contract terms.
