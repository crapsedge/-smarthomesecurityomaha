Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do I have to be a homeowner?

Both homeowners and renters in Omaha and surrounding areas may submit inquiries. Hardware and installation options depend on landlord permission and provider equipment specifications.

2. What happens after I submit my request?

Your details are routed to a participating third-party security provider serving your address. A representative will contact you directly to confirm system needs and schedule options.

3. Can I change the package or equipment?

Yes. The advertised packages represent starting options. Participating providers can customize camera counts, sensors, and home automation add-ons during your consultation.

4. Who provides the installation and monitoring?

Services, installation labor, equipment warranties, and 24/7 central-station monitoring are provided directly by participating licensed third-party security partners, not Murrays Empire LLC.

5. Are displayed prices final?

Advertised rates represent standard promotional options. Final monthly monitoring rates and installation fees depend on final qualification, equipment selection, and provider terms.
